about: Radon collects in school buildings overnight, and conventional mitigation is too costly for large, older buildings. Using radon monitors and the school's existing HVAC system, we scheduled ventilation so radon falls below the 4 pCi/L limit before staff arrive, while running the system less than a fixed schedule does. The full study is the CHASE 2025 paper.
